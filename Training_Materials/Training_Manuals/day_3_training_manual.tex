\chapter{Morning Session: Evidence Synthesis \& Briefing Architecture}

\section{The Parliamentary Evidentiary Hierarchy}
Parliamentary researchers frequently receive conflicting information from government entities. A ministry may report 100\% project completion, while the Controller and Auditor General (CAG) cites stalled works, and statistical surveys reveal widespread citizen dissatisfaction.

To protect the institutional authority of the National Assembly, researchers must evaluate evidence according to the strict **Parliamentary Evidentiary Hierarchy**:

\begin{figure}[htbp]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.94\textwidth]{figures/fig_day3_evidence_hierarchy.png}
  \caption{Hierarchy of Authoritative Evidence in Parliamentary Research.}
  \label{fig:evidence_hierarchy}
\end{figure}

\subsection{The Rule of Triangulation}
\textbf{Never base a parliamentary brief on a single ministerial claim.} If an executive agency asserts an outcome in a Tier 4 document, the researcher must command the AI to triangulate that claim against Tier 2 (CAG audits) or Tier 3 (NBS empirical data) before tabling it before a Parliamentary Standing Committee.

\section{The 4-Tier Policy Briefing Architecture}
Members of Parliament (MPs) and Committee Chairs operate under intense scheduling demands. When preparing for floor debates or committee hearings, they do not read 60-page academic treatises. They require structured, bite-sized evidence briefs.

\begin{figure}[htbp]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.94\textwidth]{figures/fig_day3_policy_architecture.png}
  \caption{Structural Framework of an Executive Parliamentary Policy Brief.}
  \label{fig:policy_architecture}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Component Breakdown}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]
    \item \textbf{Tier I: Executive Callout:} A shaded blockquote at the top of the brief. An MP who has only 90 seconds before entering the debating chamber must be able to grasp the core issue, the financial variance, and the primary question to put to the minister.
    \item \textbf{Tier II: Strategic Context:} Grounds the analysis within the Republic's strategic planning framework, linking the issue directly to the National Five-Year Development Plan (FYDP III) or the relevant Sector Strategic Plan.
    \item \textbf{Tier III: Empirical Evidence \& Tables:} Replaces discursive rhetoric with structured, tabular evidence. Percentages, variance calculations, and delivery gaps must be visible at a glance.
    \item \textbf{Tier IV: Policy Implications:} Formulates actionable insights without prescribing partisan decisions. It presents the objective consequences of executive performance.
\end{enumerate}
