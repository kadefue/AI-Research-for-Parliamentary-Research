\noindent\textbf{\textcolor{tanzaniablue}{\large 1. Executive Program Summary \& Pedagogical Philosophy}}
\vspace{0.1cm}

\noindent This executive training program equips non-programming parliamentary researchers and governance practitioners with modern, automated skills to ingest, interrogate, and synthesize authentic public documents using \textbf{Visual Studio Code} and role-grounded Artificial Intelligence assistants.

\vspace{0.15cm}
\noindent\fbox{%
    \parbox{0.98\textwidth}{%
        \small\textbf{The Zero-Manual-Copy Mandate ("Drop the File, Ask the AI"):}\\
        Manual highlighting, copying, and pasting from scanned PDFs or Word files into editors is strictly prohibited. Trainees drop native Microsoft Word (\texttt{.docx}) and Adobe PDF (\texttt{.pdf}) files directly into workspace folders and direct the AI via file-grounded commands (\texttt{@filename}) to extract tables, compare statutory clauses, reconcile audit queries, and compile executive-ready PDF briefs.
    }%
}

\noindent\textbf{\textcolor{tanzaniablue}{\large 2. Prerequisites \& Participant Profile}}
\vspace{0.1cm}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Target Trainees:} Parliamentary researchers, standing committee clerks, elected legislators, social and political scientists, and policy analysts. \textbf{No prior programming, scripting, or computer science knowledge is required or assumed.}
    \item \textbf{Required General Competencies:} Basic computer literacy (file navigation, creating folders, opening applications) and professional familiarity with parliamentary proceedings, government bills, audit reports, and national policy documents.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{-0.15cm}
\noindent\textbf{\textcolor{tanzaniablue}{\large 4. Required Participant Document Portfolio (Bring on USB / Local Disk)}}
\vspace{0.1cm}

\noindent Every participant \textbf{must arrive on Day 1} with their own live institutional dossier in digital format:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{1 Native Word Memo / Strategic Document (\texttt{.docx}):} Ministerial implementation plan, executive circular, or white paper.
    \item \textbf{1 Principal / Parent Act (\texttt{.pdf}):} A gazetted Act of Parliament (e.g., Roads Act, Water Supply Act, Land Act).
    \item \textbf{1 Draft Amendment Bill (\texttt{.docx} or \texttt{.pdf}):} A proposed amending bill containing revised clauses and statutory mandates.
    \item \textbf{1 Controller and Auditor General (CAG) Audit Report (\texttt{.pdf}):} Annual or special audit report on public authorities/LGAs.
    \item \textbf{1 Official Statistical Report (\texttt{.pdf}):} National Bureau of Statistics (NBS) census, agricultural survey, or economic abstract.
    \item \textbf{1 Sector Strategic Plan or Policy White Paper (\texttt{.pdf}):} Strategic framework aligned with FYDP III or Vision 2025/2050.
\end{enumerate}
