\chapter{Morning Session: Research Ethics and Integrity Standards}

\section{The High Stakes of Parliamentary Accuracy}
In academic research, an error or flawed citation results in a journal correction or peer criticism. In the Parliament of Tanzania, an inaccurate citation, a fabricated statutory clause, or an unverified budget variance can disrupt legislative proceedings, misdirect billions in public appropriations, or compromise constitutional oversight.

Parliamentary researchers, committee clerks, and policy analysts bear direct professional and institutional responsibility for every brief tabled before a standing committee.

\begin{figure}[htbp]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.92\textwidth]{figures/fig_day5_ethical_pillars.png}
  \caption{Ethical Framework for Parliamentary Artificial Intelligence Systems.}
  \label{fig:ethical_pillars}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Safeguarding Parliamentary Privilege and Confidentiality}
Under parliamentary law and the National Security Act, parliamentary materials fall into two distinct legal classes:
\begin{itemize}[label=-]
    \item \textbf{Public Parliamentary Documents (Permitted for Workspace Indexing):} Gazetted Acts of Parliament, officially tabled Bills, published CAG annual reports, and publicly released NBS bulletins. These documents may be ingested and queried freely in your local VS Code workspace.
    \item \textbf{Privileged and Confidential Documents (Strictly Governed):} Draft standing committee oversight findings, in-camera witness testimonies, unreleased special audit queries, and internal political party strategy papers. These files are protected by parliamentary privilege. Uploading them to public consumer AI platforms violates constitutional privilege and data privacy regulations.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Mandatory Working Rule:} Always verify that your Visual Studio Code AI environment is configured to run through certified private institutional instances or local offline models when handling unreleased committee documents.

\subsection{The Four-Step Anti-Hallucination Protocol}
Artificial intelligence models are statistical text generators, not truth engines. When queried about legal sections, an unconstrained model may fabricate realistic-sounding but non-existent sections (e.g., citing *Section 48 of the Water Supply Act* when that Act contains only 38 sections).

To maintain research integrity, execute the **Four-Step Anti-Hallucination Protocol**:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{Step \arabic*:}]
    \item \textbf{Strict Text Boundary Injection:} Always bind the model with an explicit boundary instruction:
    \begin{quote}
        \small\textit{"Rely exclusively on the text in the specified documents. If a statutory provision, financial figure, or fact is not stated in the files, state 'Not Found in Source' rather than inferring or extrapolating."}
    \end{quote}
    \item \textbf{Primary Source Cross-Verification:} Never table a brief citing an Act or Section number without opening the official Government Gazette or TanzLII repository in the VS Code split pane to verify that the section exists and reads verbatim.
    \item \textbf{Arithmetic Verification:} Always audit mathematical calculations generated by the model. If the AI asserts that budget execution reached 63.5\%, recalculate:
    \begin{equation*}
        \text{Execution Rate (\%)} = \frac{\text{Actual Expenditure}}{\text{Budget Allocation}} \times 100
    \end{equation*}
    \item \textbf{The Human Accountability Mandate:} An algorithm cannot appear before a parliamentary committee or sign an official brief. The parliamentary researcher carries full personal, professional, and legal accountability for all submitted findings.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The Standard Institutional AI Disclosure Notice}
Every document produced with AI assistance must include the official \textbf{AB Consult / COEBS Research Disclosure Notice}:

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=calloutbg, colframe=bordergrey, title=\textbf{Standard Institutional AI Disclosure Notice}]
\small
\textbf{INSTITUTIONAL METHODOLOGY \& AI DISCLOSURE NOTICE:}\\
\textit{"This parliamentary document was prepared with the technical assistance of an Artificial Intelligence system deployed within Visual Studio Code for multi-document textual indexing, data extraction, and preliminary summarization. In accordance with the Research Integrity Standards established by AB Consult, College of Economics and Business Studies (COEBS), Sokoine University of Agriculture (SUA), all empirical calculations, statutory references, and policy synthesis were independently audited, fact-checked, and verified against primary government records by Parliamentary Research Staff prior to publication."}
