# Executive Summary: Education Sector Policy and ESDP IV

The central national challenge identified in the Government’s Education and Training Policy (2014, 2023 edition) and ESDP IV is aligning an expanding education system with the country’s changing social and economic needs so it produces an appropriately skilled workforce. The policy diagnoses an overemphasis on general education, curricula that lag economic, social, scientific and technological change, weaknesses in quality regulation and certification, skills shortages, weak management, and uncertain, unsustainable financing. ESDP IV carries these issues forward as priorities for sector reform and socio-economic development. ([Policy, pp. 28–29](<../documents/education-and-training-policy-of-2014-2023-edition.pdf#page=38>); [ESDP IV, p. 29](<../documents/ESDP IV 2025-2031.pdf#page=45>))

Over its stated five-year horizon of 2025/26–2029/30, ESDP IV proposes expanding education capacity and access across pre-primary, primary, secondary, TVET and higher education; improving equity, inclusion, learner retention and gender participation; strengthening competence-based curricula, teacher recruitment and development, and labour-market-relevant vocational skills; and improving quality assurance, assessment, governance, data use and education financing. It also calls for stronger TVET-industry collaboration and recognition of skills acquired outside formal training. ([ESDP IV, pp. 29–30](<../documents/ESDP IV 2025-2031.pdf#page=45>); [pp. 46–48](<../documents/ESDP IV 2025-2031.pdf#page=62>))

The plan projects a total cost of TSh 39.352 trillion in 2023 constant prices; its financing-gap section reports an unfunded TSh 3.621 trillion (9.4%) and suggests increased education budget allocations, partner support and innovative financing. Those resource figures do not reconcile within the plan: the preceding resources table projects TSh 38.455 trillion, including TSh 4.274 trillion in partner resources, while the funding-gap table records TSh 293 billion in partner support. Statutory measures proposed by the policy include amending the Education Act, Chapter 353, on education ages and duration, compulsory education, education streams, language of instruction and quality-assurance roles; reviewing laws governing education institutions and the two local-government authorities acts; and ESDP IV calls for harmonizing higher-education laws and regulations by 2030. ([ESDP IV, pp. 68–71](<../documents/ESDP IV 2025-2031.pdf#page=84>); [Policy, pp. 77–79](<../documents/education-and-training-policy-of-2014-2023-edition.pdf#page=87>); [ESDP IV, p. 58](<../documents/ESDP IV 2025-2031.pdf#page=74>))
