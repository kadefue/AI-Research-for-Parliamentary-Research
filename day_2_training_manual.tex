\chapter{Morning Session: The CLEAR Prompting Framework}

\section{Why Conversational Chatting Fails Parliamentary Scrutiny}
When untrained researchers use AI, they often treat the system like a casual conversational partner, typing queries such as:
\begin{center}
    \textit{"Can you summarize what this amendment bill changes?"}
\end{center}
In legislative research, this approach produces severe operational risks:
\begin{itemize}[label=-]
    \item \textbf{Unanchored Generalizations:} The AI generates vague narrative summaries instead of pinpointing exact section-by-section alterations.
    \item \textbf{Loss of Statutory Sub-Clauses:} Crucial legal qualifiers (such as \textit{"subject to the written approval of the Minister"} or \textit{"shall not apply to contracts under Section 12"}) are omitted.
    \item \textbf{Partisan or Subjective Tone:} The AI adopts speculative or opinionated phrasing that violates the non-partisan code of parliamentary staff.
\end{itemize}

\section{The Five Pillars of CLEAR Prompting}
To enforce absolute rigor, \textbf{AB Consult} has established the **CLEAR Prompting Framework**. Every prompt directed at legislative files must embody all five structural elements:

\begin{figure}[htbp]
  \centering
  \framebox{\parbox{0.92\textwidth}{\centering
    \vspace{1.2cm}
    \textbf{The CLEAR Legislative Scrutiny Prompting Architecture} \\[0.3cm]
    \small
    \textbf{[C] Context \& Role} $\rightarrow$ Assign institutional stance: \textit{"Senior Parliamentary Legislative Counsel to the Standing Committee on Governance."}\\[0.15cm]
    \textbf{[L] Legislative Scope} $\rightarrow$ Pinpoint native files: \textit{"Directly examine @documents/parent\_act.pdf and @documents/amendment\_bill.docx."}\\[0.15cm]
    \textbf{[E] Evidence Boundary} $\rightarrow$ Lock extraction: \textit{"Rely exclusively on text inside these two files. If a clause is unchanged, state 'Unchanged'."}\\[0.15cm]
    \textbf{[A] Action \& Architecture} $\rightarrow$ Dictate layout: \textit{"Produce a 4-column Markdown table: Clause Element, Existing Act, Proposed Amendment, Oversight Risk."}\\[0.15cm]
    \textbf{[R] Restraint \& Neutrality} $\rightarrow$ Enforce tone: \textit{"Maintain strict parliamentary neutrality. State practical legal implications without partisan opinion."}
    \vspace{1.2cm}
  }}
  \caption{Structural Architecture of the CLEAR Prompting Protocol.}
  \label{fig:clear_framework}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Deconstructing the CLEAR Prompt Components}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]
    \item \textbf{Context \& Role (C):} Grounds the model in the parliamentary tradition. By designating the AI as a Committee Clerk or Legislative Counsel, the model adopts formal parliamentary terminology and objective scrutiny criteria.
    \item \textbf{Legislative Scope (L):} Replaces vague prompts with direct file references. By specifying \texttt{@documents/parent\_act.pdf} and \texttt{@documents/amendment\_bill.docx}, the AI searches the binary content of those exact files.
    \item \textbf{Evidence Boundary (E):} Establishes the perimeter of truth. The AI is explicitly prohibited from drawing upon outside general internet knowledge, eliminating statutory hallucinations.
    \item \textbf{Action \& Architecture (A):} Specifies the output structure: exact table headers, bullet formats, and word counts.
    \item \textbf{Restraint \& Neutrality (R):} Mandates non-partisan language. Under Standing Orders, parliamentary researchers do not declare whether a bill is "good" or "bad"; they illuminate statutory mechanics, legal risks, and implementation trade-offs.
\end{enumerate}
